## 1. Introduction

The **UNZA Decentralized Sanitation Systems Hub** is the national knowledge platform for sanitation research, policy, training, and monitoring in Zambia. It is managed by the University of Zambia Department of Civil Engineering.

### What You Can Do on This Platform

- **Browse and download** research papers, policy documents, reports, training materials, and data sets
- **Find upcoming events** — conferences, workshops, and symposia
- **Register for training sessions** on sanitation engineering and practice
- **Explore demonstration sites** — a map and directory of sanitation installations across Zambia
- **Save resources** to bookmarks and reading lists for later reference
- **Export citations** in academic formats (APA, MLA, Chicago, BibTeX)

### Who Can Access What

| Content | Anyone | Registered User | UNZA Member |
|---------|--------|----------------|-------------|
| Public resources | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| University resources | ✗ | ✗ | ✓ |
| Staff resources | ✗ | ✗ | UNZA Staff only |
| Downloading files | ✗* | ✓ | ✓ |
| Training registration | ✗ | ✓ | ✓ |
| Bookmarks & reading lists | ✗ | ✓ | ✓ |

*Downloads may require a free account depending on platform settings.

## 2. Accessing the Platform

Open your web browser and navigate to the platform URL provided by your institution (e.g., `https://hub.unza.ac.zm`).

The homepage displays:
- A search bar for finding resources quickly
- A statistics strip showing platform content counts
- Featured recent resources
- Upcoming events
- Links to key sections

You do not need an account to browse public resources.

## 3. Creating an Account

A free account gives you access to restricted resources, file downloads, bookmarks, and training registration.

### 3.1 Who Should Create an Account

- **UNZA students and staff** — use your UNZA institutional login (no registration needed)
- **External researchers and practitioners** — create a free email account
- **Government officials and NGO staff** — create a free email account

### 3.2 Registration Steps

1. Click **Register** in the top-right corner of any page
2. Select the **Register** tab in the sign-in panel
3. Fill in the registration form:
   - **Full Name** — your complete name
   - **Email Address** — your work or academic email (used for login)
   - **Password** — minimum 8 characters, mix of letters and numbers
   - **Confirm Password** — repeat your password
   - **Phone** (optional) — your contact number
   - **Affiliation** — your institution or organisation
   - **UNZA ID** (optional) — if you are a UNZA student or staff member
   - **Job Title** (optional)
4. Complete the CAPTCHA verification if shown
5. Click **Create Account**
6. Check your email for a **verification message**
7. Click the verification link in the email to activate your account
8. You can now sign in

> **Note:** If you do not receive the verification email within 5 minutes, check your spam/junk folder.

## 4. Signing In

### 4.1 UNZA Students — Student Portal Login

1. Click **Sign In**
2. Select the **Student Login** tab
3. Enter your UNZA student number and Online Academic Portal (OAP) password
4. Click **Sign In with Student Portal**

Your account is created automatically if this is your first login. No email verification is required.

### 4.2 UNZA Staff — Staff/LDAP Login

1. Click **Sign In**
2. Select the **Staff Login** tab (or **UNZA Login** if LDAP is configured)
3. Enter your UNZA ID and HR Portal password
4. Click **Sign In with Staff Portal**

### 4.3 Email Login (External Users)

1. Click **Sign In**
2. Select the **Email Login** tab
3. Enter your registered email address and password
4. Click **Sign In**

### 4.4 Sign Out

Click your name in the top-right corner, then click **Sign Out**. You are logged out immediately.

## 5. Browsing the Resource Library

Navigate to **Library** in the main navigation to browse all available resources.

### 5.1 Resource Listing

Resources are displayed as cards showing:
- **Thumbnail image** (if available)
- **Content type badge** (Report, Policy, Research, Training Material, etc.)
- **Publication date**
- **Title**
- **Summary** (first 3 lines)
- **Access level** badge (Open Access, University Access, etc.)

### 5.2 Filtering Resources

Use the filters on the left side (or top on mobile) to narrow results:

| Filter | Options |
|--------|---------|
| Content Type | Report, Policy, Journal Article, Training Material, Dataset, etc. |
| Category | Sanitation Policy, Water Quality, Hygiene Promotion, etc. |
| Department | UNZA department filter |
| Access Level | Public only, All accessible |

Filters can be combined. Results update as you select filters.

### 5.3 Sorting Results

Sort the resource list by:
- Most Recent (default)
- Most Viewed
- Title (A-Z)
- Publication Date

### 5.4 Pagination

Resources are displayed in pages. Use the **Previous** and **Next** buttons at the bottom to navigate between pages.

---

## 6. Searching for Resources

### 6.1 Quick Search

Use the **search bar** on the homepage or at the top of the Library page.

Type your query and press **Enter** or click **Search**. The system searches across:
- Resource titles
- Summaries and body text
- Keywords and tags
- Author names
- Publisher names

### 6.2 Advanced Search Tips

- **Phrase search:** Put quotes around a phrase — `"faecal sludge management"` finds that exact phrase
- **Multiple terms:** Separate terms with spaces — `biodigester zambia 2023` finds resources containing all these words
- **Partial words:** The search automatically finds related forms — `sanitation` also finds `sanitations`

### 6.3 Browsing by Category

From the main navigation, click **Categories** to see a visual grid of all content categories. Click any category to see all resources in that area.

---

## 7. Viewing a Resource

Click any resource title or card to open the full resource detail page.

### 7.1 Resource Information Panel

The detail page shows:
- **Title and type** — e.g., "National Sanitation Policy 2022 · Policy Document"
- **Summary** — brief description
- **Full body text** (if available)
- **Metadata panel** on the right side:
  - Authors / Contributors
  - Publisher
  - Publication Date
  - Language
  - External Identifier (DOI, ISBN)
  - Keywords
  - Category
  - View count

### 7.2 Attached Files

Below the resource body, the **Files** section lists all attached documents, datasets, or media.

Each file shows:
- File name
- File type and size
- **Preview** button — opens the file in your browser (for PDFs and images)
- **Download** button — saves the file to your computer

### 7.3 Citing a Resource

Click **Cite** (or the citation icon) to export the reference in your preferred format:
- **APA** — American Psychological Association
- **MLA** — Modern Language Association
- **Chicago** — Chicago Manual of Style
- **BibTeX** — For LaTeX users

Click the **Copy** button to copy the citation to your clipboard.

### 7.4 Bookmarking a Resource

Click the **Bookmark** icon (star/flag icon) on the resource detail page to save it to your bookmarks. Bookmarks are accessible from your profile.

You must be signed in to use bookmarks.

### 7.5 Related Resources

At the bottom of each resource page, you may see **Related Resources** — other content in the same category or with similar keywords.

---

## 8. Downloading Files

### 8.1 How to Download

1. Open the resource detail page
2. Scroll to the **Files** section
3. Click **Download** next to the file you want

If you are not signed in and downloads require login, you will be prompted to sign in first.

### 8.2 Download Limits

There are no download limits per user. You may download any resource you have access to as many times as needed.

### 8.3 File Previews

For PDF files, click **Preview** to view the document directly in your browser without downloading. Other file types (Word, Excel, etc.) will download automatically.

## 9. Events & Training

### 9.1 Upcoming Events

Navigate to **Events** from the main navigation to see all upcoming conferences, workshops, seminars, and symposia.

Each event shows:
- Title and organiser
- Date(s) and venue
- Description
- Contact information
- External registration link (if available)

### 9.2 Training Sessions

Navigate to **Training** to see available capacity-building sessions.

Each session shows:
- Title and instructor
- Mode (Online, In-Person, Hybrid)
- Venue and dates
- Available places (if capacity is limited)
- Requirements/prerequisites

### 9.3 Registering for a Training Session

1. Navigate to **Training**
2. Click on a session to view details
3. Click **Register for This Session** (you must be signed in)
4. You receive a confirmation notification

### 9.4 Cancelling Registration

1. Go to your **Profile**
2. Click the **Training** tab
3. Find the session and click **Cancel Registration**

### 9.5 Viewing Your Registrations

1. Sign in to your account
2. Go to **Profile → Training**
3. You will see all sessions you have registered for, with their current status

## 10. Demonstration Sites

Navigate to **Demonstration Sites** from the main navigation to explore the registry of sanitation installations across Zambia.

### 10.1 Site Listing

Sites are displayed in a list/grid with:
- Site name and type

If map coordinates are available, sites can be displayed on an interactive map showing their geographic distribution across Zambia's districts.

## 11. Your Account & Bookmarks

### 11.1 Accessing Your Profile

Click your name or avatar in the top-right corner, then click **Profile**.

Your profile page shows:
- Your personal information
- Account details (email, registration date, account type)
- Tabs for: Bookmarks, Recently Viewed, Reading Lists, Training Registrations

### 11.2 Editing Your Profile

1. Go to **Profile**
2. Click **Edit Profile**
3. Update: Full Name, Phone, Institution, Job Title
4. Click **Save**

> UNZA institutional users (LDAP, Student Portal, HR Portal) cannot change their name or email — these are managed by UNZA systems.

### 11.3 Bookmarks

Your bookmarks are resources you have saved for quick access.

**To bookmark a resource:**
1. Open the resource
2. Click the bookmark icon

**To view bookmarks:**
- Go to **Profile → Bookmarks**

**To remove a bookmark:**
- Click the bookmark icon again on the resource, or
- Click **Remove** in your bookmarks list

### 11.4 Recently Viewed

The **Recently Viewed** tab shows the last resources you opened. This helps you quickly return to content you were working with.

### 11.5 Reading Lists

Reading lists are personal collections of resources you organise yourself.

**Creating a reading list:**
1. Go to **Profile → Reading Lists**
2. Click **New Reading List**
3. Enter a name and description
4. Choose whether to make it **Public** (anyone can view it) or **Private**

**Adding resources to a reading list:**
1. Open a resource
2. Click **Add to Reading List**
3. Select the reading list from the dropdown

## 12. Notifications

The system sends you in-platform notifications for events like:
- Content you submitted has been approved or returned
- Your account status changed
- Welcome message on first login

**Viewing notifications:**
1. Click the **bell icon** in the top navigation bar
2. The number badge shows unread notifications

**Marking as read:**
- Click any notification to mark it as read
- Click **Mark All Read** to clear all notifications

## 13. Password Reset

### 13.1 If You Forgot Your Password

1. Go to the Sign In page
2. Click **Forgot Password?** below the login form
3. Enter your registered email address
4. Click **Send Reset Link**
5. Check your email for the reset link
6. Click the link in the email (valid for 1 hour)
7. Enter and confirm your new password
8. Click **Reset Password**

You will be redirected to the login page. Sign in with your new password.

### 13.2 Changing Your Password While Logged In

1. Go to **Profile**
2. Click **Change Password** or the security tab
3. Enter your current password
4. Enter and confirm your new password
5. Click **Save**

> UNZA institutional accounts (LDAP, Student Portal, HR Portal) — password changes must be done through the respective UNZA system, not here.

## 14. Access Levels Explained

Different resources have different access levels. If you cannot access a resource, it may require a higher access level.

| Badge Shown | Meaning |
|-------------|---------|
| **Open Access** | Available to everyone — no login required |
| **University Access** | Requires a UNZA account (student or staff) |
| **Staff Only** | Requires a UNZA staff account |
| **Core Team** | Administrator access only |

If you see a resource but cannot download or view it, you may need to:
1. **Sign in** — if you are not logged in
2. **Use institutional login** — if it requires a UNZA account
3. **Contact the administrator** — if you believe you should have access

## 15. Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: I registered but cannot sign in. What should I do?

**A:** Check your email for a verification message and click the link inside. If you did not receive it, check your spam folder. If you still cannot find it, contact the system administrator.

---

### Q: I am a UNZA student. Which login should I use?

**A:** Use the **Student Login** tab and enter your UNZA student number and OAP (Online Academic Portal) password — the same credentials you use for student registration and results.

---

### Q: I am UNZA staff. Which login should I use?

**A:** Use the **Staff Login** tab if available, or the **UNZA Login / LDAP** tab. Use your UNZA staff ID and HR portal credentials.

---

### Q: Why can I see a resource but not download it?

**A:** Downloading may require a login. Sign in with any account type. If you are already signed in and still cannot download, the file may be restricted to specific user groups. Contact the system administrator.

---

### Q: How do I cite a resource in my paper?

**A:** Open the resource detail page and click the **Cite** button. Select your citation format (APA, MLA, Chicago, or BibTeX) and copy the generated citation.

---

### Q: Can I upload my own research to the platform?

**A:** Yes, if you are a UNZA staff member or have been granted content creation access. Sign in with your UNZA institutional account, then contact the system administrator to request content management access.

---

### Q: I cannot find a resource I am looking for. What should I do?

**A:** Try the following:
1. Use the **Search** function with different keywords
2. Browse by **Category** rather than searching
3. Check that you are signed in (some content is restricted)
4. Contact the administrator if you believe the resource should be on the platform

---

### Q: How do I suggest a resource for inclusion in the library?

**A:** Contact the platform team using the email address listed on the **Contact** page, or email the UNZA Department of Civil Engineering directly.

---

### Q: Is my personal data secure?

**A:** Yes. The platform uses industry-standard security: all passwords are stored as secure hashes (bcrypt), all communication is encrypted (HTTPS), and access tokens expire after 12 hours. UNZA institutional users log in through their existing secure UNZA credentials.

---

### Q: I forgot which email I registered with. What can I do?

**A:** If you are a UNZA student or staff, try using the institutional login tabs instead — they do not require an email address. If you used email registration, contact the system administrator with your full name and they can look up your account.

---

### Q: How do I update my profile picture?

**A:** The current version uses initials-based avatars generated from your name. Profile photo uploads are not yet supported.
